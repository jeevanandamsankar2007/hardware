01 / WHO WE ARE

ENGINEERING THE DETAILS THAT MAKE THE DIFFERENCE.

FORGEX INDUSTRIES manufactures heavy-duty industrial hardware, structural fasteners, precision-machined shafts, and custom engineered components engineered to withstand high-stress operating environments.

Founded on principles of strict metallurgical integrity and relentless machining discipline, our facilities combine advanced CNC lathe turning, 5-axis milling centers, multi-station cold heading, and precision laser fabrication. Whether fulfilling standardized bulk fastener runs or producing complex proprietary components to exact customer drawings, our engineering team ensures dimensional exactitude, dependable batch consistency, and verified traceability from raw billet to final packaged shipment.

05 / QUALITY

QUALITY IS BUILT INTO EVERY STEP.

Our quality control protocols monitor components at every stage of production to guarantee adherence to drawings and international hardware standards.

Incoming Material Inspection

Verification of raw steel bar stock and sheets against Mill Test Reports (MTR) with optical spectrometry and metallurgical grade validation.

Dimensional Inspection

Bridge Coordinate Measuring Machine (CMM) probing, laser profile scanning, and calibrated micrometer inspection across critical features.

In-Process Monitoring

Statistical Process Control (SPC) charting, first-article sample approvals, and scheduled tool-wear offsets during continuous production runs.

Final Batch Inspection

AQL sampling plans verifying plating thickness, thread go/no-go gauging, surface roughness (Ra), and destructive tensile proof testing.

Complete Batch Traceability

Full serialization and heat-number tracking linking every finished component back to the specific furnace heat and machine log.

Packaging & Dispatch Checks

Vapor Corrosion Inhibitor (VCI) preservation, moisture-sealed barrier wrapping, and heavy-duty reinforced shipping containment.

COMPANY ETHOS // FORGEX STORY

BUILT FROM THE MACHINE SHOP FLOOR UP.

FORGEX INDUSTRIES was founded by precision tooling engineers who recognized an urgent deficit in the industrial component supply chain: commercial clients were continually forced to compromise between micro-tolerance precision and high-volume delivery speeds.

By consolidating heavy cold-heading lines, 5-axis CNC machining centers, robotic welding fabrication, and a climate-controlled optical inspection laboratory under a unified quality management umbrella, FORGEX provides true single-source responsibility. Every component is produced with verifiable material certifications, measured to drawing tolerances, and dispatched on scheduled logistical timetables.
